Supplication (dua) is a vital practice: an intimate expression of need, dependence, and hope before God. The Prophet ﷺ taught many duas for morning, evening, travel, and hardship—each with its etiquette and beauty.

Etiquette begins with praising God and sending blessings upon the Prophet, making clear and humble requests, and persisting with patience. Dua should be sincere, specific, and accompanied by lawful means. Pairing dua with action—planning, effort, and counsel—demonstrates trust without passivity.

Selected authentic duas cover guidance, protection, forgiveness, and provision. Memorizing short, meaningful supplications fosters readiness in everyday life and moments of crisis.
