title: Youth, Contemporary Fitnah, and Islamic Guidance
image: https://via.placeholder.com/1200x400?text=Youth
---

Youth face rapid social change, digital influence, and identity pressures. Islamic guidance offers anchors: rooted faith, community mentorship, and critical engagement with ideas. Education should address real-life concerns—relationships, career ethics, and civic participation—while fostering resilience.

Programs that combine spiritual formation with skills training help youth contribute positively to society. Media literacy and mentorship counter harmful influences, and communal spaces provide safe dialogue.
