---
title: Pursuit of Knowledge: Manners and Importance
image: https://via.placeholder.com/1200x400?text=Knowledge
---

Seeking knowledge is highly esteemed in Islam; the Prophet ﷺ said seeking knowledge is obligatory. Knowledge cultivates ethical discernment and community benefit. Manners include humility, verifying sources, teaching others, and acting on what one learns.

Practical tips: balance study with service, combine religious and worldly learning, and mentor others. Knowledge should transform character and benefit society.

References:
- Hadith on seeking knowledge (Ibn Majah; Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn)
- Quran: Al-ʿAlaq 96:1–5
