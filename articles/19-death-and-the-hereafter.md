---
title: Death and the Hereafter: Preparing Life for Afterlife
image: https://via.placeholder.com/1200x400?text=Afterlife
---

Islamic eschatology emphasizes accountability and moral preparedness: belief in resurrection, judgment, paradise, and hell motivates ethical urgency. Life is a trust and test; remembering death encourages repentance, charity, and will-making.

Practical steps: prepare legal wills, settle debts, care for the terminally ill, and prioritize deeds that benefit one after death (sadaqah jariyah). Such practices align daily life with long-term spiritual goals.

References:
- Quran: Al-Qiyamah; Al-Mulk
- Hadith: collections on death and the grave (Bukhari; Muslim)
