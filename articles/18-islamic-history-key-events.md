Islamic history offers instructive episodes: the Hijra (migration) teaches community-building under pressure; the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah shows strategic patience; early public welfare reforms point to governance principles. Studying these events cultivates humility and practical lessons for leadership and justice.

Use reliable chronicles (Tabari, Ibn Kathir) and modern scholarship to understand context and extract applicable lessons for today.

References:
- al-Tabari; Ibn Kathir; classical seerah works
