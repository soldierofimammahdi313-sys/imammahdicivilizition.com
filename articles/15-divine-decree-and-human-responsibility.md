---
title: Belief in Divine Decree and Human Responsibility
image: https://via.placeholder.com/1200x400?text=Qadar
---

Islam affirms belief in divine decree (qadaʾ wa-qadar) while maintaining human moral responsibility. God’s knowledge and will encompass all events; yet humans exercise choice and are accountable. This balance fosters trust in God during trials and underscores ethical striving.

Practically, believers practice patience (sabr), accept hardships with faith, and pursue remedies and effort. Fatalism is rejected; planning and responsible action remain essential.

References:
- Quran: Al-Qamar 54:49; Al-Insan 76:30
- Hadith and creedal discussions on qadar
