---
title: Rights of Parents and Filial Conduct
image: https://via.placeholder.com/1200x400?text=Parents
---

Islam places great emphasis on honoring parents. The Quran repeatedly commands kindness toward parents, especially in old age, equating filial gratitude with gratitude to God. Duties include respectful speech, caring for their needs, and seeking their counsel while refusing commands that require disobedience to God.

Family life sometimes involves difficult tensions when parental wishes conflict with ethical or religious obligations. Islam balances obedience with moral limits: do not obey if it entails wrongdoing. Compassion, patience, and efforts toward reconciliation are encouraged.

Practical care: regular visits, shared decisions, care plans for aging parents, and using community services. When conflict escalates, mediation and counseling grounded in Islamic ethics can provide resolution.

References:
- Quran: Al-Isra 17:23–24; Luqman 31:14–15
- Hadith: Sahih al-Bukhari; Sahih Muslim
